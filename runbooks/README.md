# Runbooks

Practical, field-tested IT and security runbooks. Plain English. Built for small-to-mid teams, MSPs, and one-person shops who need to act fast without enterprise-tier tooling.

## Available runbooks

- **[Cybersecurity Incident Response](./cybersecurity-incident-response.md)** — Universal IR playbook covering the full NIST 800-61r2 lifecycle plus scenario quick plays for ransomware, BEC, malware, phishing, exfiltration, DDoS, lost devices, and web app compromise.
- **[Compliance Runbook](./compliance-runbook.md)** — Cross-framework reference for HIPAA, HITECH, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST 800-53, NIST 800-171/CMMC, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR, and CCPA — with control mappings, audit prep checklists, and an annual compliance calendar.

## How these are written

- **Action-first**: every section is something you'd actually do, not theory
- **Plain English**: regulations and frameworks translated, not quoted verbatim
- **Open, not proprietary**: no vendor lock-in, no "buy my product" links
- **Maintained**: dated at the bottom; reviewed at least quarterly

## Disclaimer

These are practitioner notes, not legal or audit advice. Compliance is fact-specific — work with qualified counsel and certified assessors for binding decisions.
